# Round 2 key (never shown to a user agent)

1-3. As KEY.md questions 5, 6 and 7.
4. Four, in this order: Arthur (7 Sept), Seward (10 Sept), Van Helsing (13 Sept), Quincey Morris (18 Sept). All are in Seward's diary (e0100, e0104, e0107, e0116). Score: 2 for all four in order, 1 for three or for four out of order.
5. From Haarlem, grown in the glass-houses of Van Helsing's friend Vanderpool. Van Helsing telegraphed for them and decked the room (e0105.2, 11 Sept; Lucy's 17 Sept diary says a boxful arrives daily from Haarlem).
6. Jonathan Harker's journal, the entry of 4-5 May (e0004.1): the Slovak and Servian words for "either were-wolf or vampire".
7. One letter only: 25 May, to Arthur Holmwood (e0030), inviting him to a camp-fire evening with Jack Seward. Everything else he "says" is quoted by others. Score: 2 for exactly the one letter, 1 for the letter plus others' quotations presented as his own writing.
